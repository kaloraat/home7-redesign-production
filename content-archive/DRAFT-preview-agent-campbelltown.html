What makes the best real estate agent in Campbelltown?

There is no single best agent for everyone. The best agent for you is the one who can get the right result for your property, at a fair price, and keep you informed the whole way. Look for these five things:

  1. Real local results. Ask which homes they have sold or leased in Campbelltown, Leumeah, Minto, Ingleburn or your own street in the past year. Recent local results beat glossy brochures.
  2. An honest price estimate. A good agent bases their estimate on comparable recent sales and shows you the evidence. Be wary of a price that seems too good to be true. It may just be designed to win your listing.
  3. Clear, fair fees. Commission, marketing and any extras should be in writing before you sign. See our full guide to real estate agent fees in Liverpool and Campbelltown.
  4. Communication you can rely on. You should know who your agent is, how to reach them, and how often you will get updates. Weekly feedback from buyers or tenants is a fair minimum.
  5. Reviews that mention the person, not just the brand. Read Google reviews and look for comments about honesty, responsiveness and negotiation.

Questions to ask a Campbelltown agent before you sign

  • What have you sold or leased near my property recently, and for how much?
  • How did you arrive at your price estimate for my home?
  • What is your commission, does it include GST, and what will marketing cost?
  • Who will I actually be dealing with: you, or someone else in the office?
  • How will you find buyers or tenants beyond the online portals?
  • How often will you update me, and how?
  • What happens if I want to end the agreement?

Remember that in NSW you have a cooling-off period on a residential sales agency agreement. You can cancel it in writing until 5pm on the next business day or Saturday after signing.

Red flags to watch for

  • A price estimate far above every other agent's, with no comparable sales to back it up.
  • Pressure to sign on the spot.
  • Marketing costs described as "to be confirmed".
  • You meet the senior agent once, then get handed to someone junior.
  • Calls and emails that go unanswered for days.
