If you are thinking of selling in Liverpool, one of the first questions is simple: how much will it actually cost? The answer depends on your property, your agent and your loan, but the costs fall into a handful of clear groups. This guide walks through each one, shows a worked example, and explains where you can save.

It is written by Home7 Real Estate, a family-owned agency in the Liverpool CBD that publishes its own fees, so you can see exactly how the numbers compare.

The main costs of selling a house in Liverpool

  1. Agent commission. Usually the biggest cost. Liverpool agents charge about 1.9% to 2% of the sale price on average, including GST. Home7 charges 1% + GST (1.1% including GST).
  2. Marketing and advertising. Listings on realestate.com.au and Domain, professional photography, floor plans, signboards and sometimes video. A premium realestate.com.au listing alone can cost a few thousand dollars, and most agents charge marketing separately from commission.
  3. Conveyancing or legal fees. A conveyancer or solicitor prepares the contract of sale and handles settlement. Get a fixed-price quote early, because the contract must be ready before your home goes on the market.
  4. Paying out your home loan. If you have a mortgage, your lender will usually charge a discharge fee, and there is a government fee to register the discharge. Ask your lender about any break costs on a fixed-rate loan.
  5. Preparing the home. Small repairs, painting, gardening, cleaning and sometimes styling. These are optional, but they often pay for themselves in the sale price.
  6. Moving costs. Removalists, storage and, if you are buying at the same time, possibly bridging finance.

Some costs are shared at settlement. Council and water rates are adjusted so you only pay for the time you owned the property. And in NSW the buyer, not the seller, pays stamp duty.

How to keep more of your sale price

  • Compare commission in dollars, not just percentages. Half a percent on a $900,000 sale is $4,500.
  • Ask whether quotes include GST. "2% plus GST" is really 2.2%.
  • Get marketing quoted in writing, and choose a campaign that suits your property rather than the most expensive package.
  • Don't choose on fee alone. An agent who prices honestly and negotiates well can make a bigger difference than the fee. Check recent local results and Google reviews.
  • Fix small things before listing. A tidy garden, fresh paint and repaired fittings are cheap compared with a lower offer.

Selling an investment property

If the property is not your main residence, capital gains tax may apply when you sell. Selling costs, including agent commission and marketing, are generally taken into account when working out your capital gain. Talk to your accountant before you list, because timing can affect the tax you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to sell a house in Liverpool NSW?

It depends mostly on agent commission, which is the largest cost. In our worked example for a $900,000 house, total costs come to about $24,000 with a typical 2% agent and about $15,900 with Home7's 1% + GST commission, once marketing, conveyancing and loan costs are added.

Do sellers pay stamp duty in NSW?

No. Stamp duty (transfer duty) is paid by the buyer. Sellers pay agent, marketing, legal and loan-related costs.

What is the average real estate commission in Liverpool?

Agent comparison data puts the Liverpool average at about 1.9% to 2% of the sale price, including GST. Home7 Real Estate charges 1% + GST, which is 1.1% including GST.

Is marketing included in agent commission?

Usually not. Most agents, including Home7, charge marketing and advertising separately from commission, and quote it in writing before the campaign starts.

Can I sell my house without an agent?

Yes, private sales are legal in NSW, but you take on pricing, marketing, inspections and negotiation yourself. With a low-commission agent, the saving from going it alone is much smaller.
